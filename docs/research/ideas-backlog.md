## Not recommended

- Back view with SD plus a dual-side reconstruction (Tailor3D). It needs a model we do not have. TripoSR takes one image, so a second view has nothing to feed. The report should say the back side is a model guess.
- Training-free control (Ctrl-X, FreeControl, OmniControlNet). 176.31 s per image on an A6000 for FreeControl (Table 2, p. 7472) and 11.51 GiB for Ctrl-X on SDXL. Breaks the 100 s limit and the 4 GB GPU.
- Native 3D diffusion or multi-view diffusion models (Direct3D, Hunyuan3D 1.0). Heavy, and Hunyuan3D is already ruled out by D18.
